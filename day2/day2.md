## prompt engineering
Just as communication is the key to success in human relationships, the same principle applies when interacting with a chatbot. Providing a clear, precise prompt ensures you receive more accurate answers. This not only saves time but also unlocks the AI's full potential. At its core, an effective prompt must contain three vital elements: context, constraints, and a clear goal.📝
